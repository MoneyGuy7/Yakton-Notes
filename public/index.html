    About Free Online Notepad

    Yakton Notes runs entirely in your browser. Your notes are saved on this device as you type and are never uploaded. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Use Backup to download all notes as a file and Restore to load them back on any device.

    FAQ

    Do I need an account?

    No. Just open the page and type.

    Where are my notes stored?

    Only in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Backup to keep a copy.

    Will I lose notes if I clear browser data?

    Yes, clearing site data erases them, so download a backup now and then.
